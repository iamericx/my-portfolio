About me

The person behind the pixels.

EK

Hi, I'm Eric — a designer who started this journey right after finishing my studies. Design wasn't something I drifted into; it's what I went looking for once school was done, and graphic design was my first real step into the field.

I spent my early days learning the fundamentals of graphic design — composition, color, typography, the kind of thinking that makes a static image actually communicate something. But the more I designed, the more I wanted to see my work live and breathing on an actual screen, not just as a flat file. That curiosity pushed me to teach myself front-end development — HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — so I could build the interfaces I was designing, not just hand them off.

That combination is still how I work today: I think like a designer first, then build like a developer, so nothing gets lost between the idea and the finished website.
